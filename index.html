About LaFamilia

Management Scholars

Building community, advancing visibility, and shaping the future of Latinx scholars in business schools since 2019.

VISION

Nuestra Visión

La Familia of Management Scholars will be a vibrant community of academics nationally recognized for providing mentorship and professional development opportunities for faculty, post-doctorates, doctoral students, and scholars. LaFamilia is open to all scholars regardless of identity who have interest in Latinx research. We aim to lead and inspire undergraduate and graduate students to thrive and become role models by pursuing academic careers.

LaFamilia is open to all scholars regardless of identity who have interest in Latinx research.

MISSION

La Misión

La Familia of Management Scholars connects faculty, post doctorate, and doctoral students navigating a U.S. context, to collaborate on Latinx research, teaching and success of all students by nurturing and providing support through culturally relevant mentorship programs and professional development opportunities.

HOW WE WORK

Our Pillars of Success

Nuestros Pilares del Éxito

1

Pillar One
Conectar

  • Connect — Maximize online presence to increase visibility and access to our community.
  • Build Community — Monthly Happy Hour, Social/Salsa events, and Cafecitos/meet-ups.
  • Ongoing Communication — Monthly Celebraciones y Noticias with accomplishments and opportunities.
2

Pillar Two
Investigar & Mentorear

  • Research — PDWs, symposia, research circles, and presentations at national and regional conferences.
  • Mentoring — Culturally relevant mentoring programs, webinars, and career development coaching.
3

Pillar Three
Inspirar

Bring commitment to Hispanic Excellence into the classroom by serving as role models, sharing our stories, inspiring students, and providing career advice and coaching.

OUR JOURNEY

La Historia / The History

From the Committee for Hispanic Excellence to a thriving national community of scholars.

2015

Committee for Hispanic Excellence (CHE)

In recognition of the Anniversary of the White House Initiative on Educational Excellence for Hispanics, The PhD Project launched the Committee for Hispanic Excellence (CHE).

2019

LaFamilia Management Scholars Established

La Familia Management Scholars was formally established as a community dedicated to Latinx scholars in management.

2020

First Virtual Gatherings

  • PDW at the Academy of Management Conference (Virtual): “Hermandad”: A Latinx Career Development Workshop
  • Monthly Happy Hours launched to connect LaFamilia
2021

Expansion & National Recognition

  • PDW at the Academy of Management Conference (Virtual): Filling the Void: Researching Our Latinx Experience
  • Expanded to include other business disciplines, forming La Familia Business Scholars under CHE — aligning with the White House’s enhanced vision for the Latinx/Hispanic American community
2022

Regional & National Presence

  • PDW at the Western Academy of Management Conference (Hawaii): Resilience and Hermandad Among Latinx Scholars
  • PDW at the Academy of Management Conference (Seattle): Doing Latinx Research and Being Latinx Researchers
2023

Putting the Latinx Worker Front and Center

  • PDW at Eastern Academy of Management (Philadelphia): Resilience and Hermandad Among Latinx Scholars
  • PDW at the Academy of Management Conference (Boston): Putting the Latinx Worker Front and Center
  • Sí Se Puede Symposium: Supporting Latina Faculty Workers to Succeed in Academia
2024–25

Special Issue Published

Equality, Diversity and Inclusion: An International Journal — Special Issue: We Are Here (Estamos Aquí): Researching the Latinx Work Experience in the United States (published June 2025).
